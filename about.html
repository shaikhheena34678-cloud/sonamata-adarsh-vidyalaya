Our Story

Educating Minds,
Inspiring Hearts

Sonamata Adarsh Vidyalaya is committed to providing quality education with Spiritual, strong moral values, discipline and holistic development. Our journey reflects our dedication towards empowering students and contributing to society.

1968

Sonamata Adarsh (Kanya) Vidyalaya, Solapur was established on 27 March 1968 by Vedmaharshi Somayaji Ahita Agni, Rishitulya Vajpayee Nanashri Atre.

1968

The school was established with the noble objective of providing educational opportunities to girls from aspiring and economically disadvantaged sections of the community.

2011

Since 2011, the school has been offering co-education by admitting both boys and girls, evolving into an institution of inclusive education.

Today

Today, the school focuses on academic excellence, moral values, discipline, sports, cultural activities and the overall development of students.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, disciplined and creative learning environment where students receive quality education, develop strong values and gain the confidence to achieve their goals.

Our Vision

To create confident, responsible and successful individuals who contribute positively to society and the nation through knowledge, values and holistic development.
